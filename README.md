# n8n Lead Capture & CRM Sync

An n8n automation workflow that automatically captures customer form submissions, cleans and organizes the data, and stores structured leads in Google Sheets.

## 🚀 Workflow

Customer submits form
↓
n8n receives the submission
↓
Data is validated and cleaned
↓
Lead information is organized
↓
Structured lead is added to Google Sheets

## 💡 Problem

Small businesses often receive customer enquiries through online forms but still have to manually copy, clean, and organize the information.

This workflow removes that repetitive manual work.
